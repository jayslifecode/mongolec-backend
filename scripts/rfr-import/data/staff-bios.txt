Badral YondonExecutive Director
Badral is the living encyclopedia of Mongolia and leads the Rally for Rangers team in Mongolia and internationally. His career in tourism began as a university student, when he worked as a hotel porter in Ulaanbaatar during Mongolia’s democratic revolution in 1990. Since then, he has become a leader in Mongolia’s tourism industry, with a deep commitment to sharing the country’s culture and heritage while protecting its unique biodiversity. As a founding member of Mongolia Quest, Badral has explored much of Mongolia’s vast countryside and shared his knowledge of its culture, landscapes, and biodiversity with hundreds of visitors over the past 25 years.
Wesley ThornberryCo-Founder
Wesley is a founding member of Rally for Rangers. Since its inception, he has helped spearhead the Rally for Rangers initiative, raising international awareness of Mongolia’s national parks and riding in every rally. Wesley is the founder of Trend Digital, which specializes in post-production imaging services for e-commerce clothing and fashion retailers. He is also a photographer, adventurer, and traveler who has documented cultures and communities from the tribes of the Omo Valley in Ethiopia to the nomadic shamans of Mongolia. Wesley currently lives in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia.
Tom MedemaCo-Founder
Tom developed a passion for national parks at a young age through family vacations throughout the American West. That passion grew into a career as a park ranger spanning nearly 30 years. Tom is equally passionate about national parks and public lands around the world and has worked with parks and monuments in South America and Europe as a subject matter expert in interpretation and education. He is excited and humbled to support the protection of natural and cultural heritage in parks and protected areas around the world. An avid sports and outdoor enthusiast, Tom spends much of his free time on two wheels, riding mountain bikes, road bikes, and motorcycles. Over the past 20 years, he has owned Harley-Davidson and BMW motorcycles and currently rides an F800GS on the back roads and trails of Maryland, Virginia, and West Virginia.
Orkhon TsolmonManager
Orkhon can fix almost any problem and make just about anything happen in the field. She is the magic behind the Rally for Rangers Mongolia team. Before joining the Mongol Ecology Center in 2014, Orkhon worked in the tourism industry for over a decade as the manager of the Baigali Uud tourist camp in Hatgal Village, Hovsgol Province. Today, she leads logistics and planning for Mongol Ecology Center programs, making sure everything comes together smoothly both on and off the road.
